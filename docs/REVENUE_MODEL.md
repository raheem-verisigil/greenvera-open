# GreenVera commercial model

## Commercial thesis

GOTI and the OpenSpace SDK are adoption surfaces, not the primary monetisation
unit. GreenVera earns revenue by operating trusted workflows around portable
evidence: collection, validation, permissions, lineage, monitoring, review
coordination and integrations.

Open interfaces reduce integration friction and create ecosystem reach.
Customers pay for secure operations, scale, workflow automation, service
levels, governance and measurable reductions in evidence handling effort.

## Initial paid product: Evidence Operations

Start with one narrow workflow: recycled-material and packaging evidence
across a buyer, selected suppliers, one product family and agreed production
batches.

Paid capabilities:
- Supplier evidence requests, reminders and reusable supplier submissions.
- Evidence intake, source-linked extraction and quality checks.
- Product, facility, batch and shipment lineage.
- Permissioned sharing with expiry, revocation and audit history.
- Evidence expiry, contradiction and correction alerts.
- Buyer-ready evidence exports and integration support.

Supplier declarations, automated checks and independent reviews must remain
distinct record types. Do not sell an assurance conclusion GreenVera has not
independently established.

## Second paid product: OpenSpace agent assurance

Target teams operating agents with consequential tool permissions or
regulated workflows. Monetise:
- Agent inventory and version/change records.
- Evaluation orchestration and retained test evidence.
- Policy-based release gates and approval workflows.
- Runtime monitoring, incident impact and reassessment.
- Private deployment, tenant controls, retention and audit exports.

Customers retain control of models, harnesses and sensitive telemetry.
Customer prompts and traces are not used for cross-customer training by default.

## Revenue streams

1. Annual platform subscriptions, priced by active workflows, suppliers,
   products/batches or managed agents—not by opaque trust scores.
2. Usage fees for evidence processing, API volume and monitored executions,
   with transparent limits and predictable overages.
3. Enterprise deployment, SSO, private networking, retention and support.
4. Paid implementation and integration services.
5. Optional independent-review coordination, with reviewer identity,
   competence, independence and scope disclosed. Fees must never buy a
   favourable result.

## Initial packaging hypothesis

- **Paid pilot:** fixed-scope 6–8 week deployment, one workflow, one buyer,
  selected suppliers and agreed success measures.
- **Growth:** annual subscription for repeatable supplier evidence workflows
  or agent-assurance lifecycle management.
- **Enterprise:** negotiated annual contract for multiple business units,
  private deployment, integrations, controls and service commitments.

Prices are intentionally not published here. Validate willingness to pay,
procurement requirements, delivery cost and measurable customer value with
real buyers before setting price points.

## Pilot success measures

For evidence operations, baseline and measure:
- Time from evidence request to complete evidence pack.
- Supplier response and completion rates.
- Share of evidence with source, scope, issuer and validity dates.
- Rework and duplicate-request rate.
- Time to trace a correction to affected products and batches.
- Buyer review time and unresolved evidence gaps.

For agent assurance, measure:
- Evaluation coverage of critical tools and permissions.
- Critical failure detection before release.
- Time to produce an auditable release decision.
- Drift/incident detection and reassessment time.
- Share of high-impact actions with attributable approval.

## 90-day commercial sequence

1. Select one design partner and one narrow workflow; avoid broad vertical
   expansion before proving demand.
2. Agree a paid pilot scope, baseline, success criteria, data boundaries and
   named decision owner.
3. Deliver with human review and exportable evidence records.
4. Convert to an annual contract only when measured value and recurring need
   are demonstrated.
5. Publish anonymised case evidence only with written customer permission.

## Commercial guardrails

- No fabricated customers, logos, endorsements, certifications or case studies.
- Schema validity does not prove environmental truth or agent safety.
- Never sell a favourable assessment or assurance grade.
- Keep the core interface open; charge for hosted operations and enterprise
  capabilities, not permission to read the specification.
- Do not ingest confidential supplier or agent data into shared training.
- Make security, privacy, data residency, retention and deletion commitments
  explicit before accepting live customer data.
